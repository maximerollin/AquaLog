# Launcher icon: underwater scene (B2)

Replaces the current icon (a front-facing tank outline floating on a dark square, generated by
`tools/design/generate_launcher_icon.py`). The palette and the "aquarium with a fish" idea stay; the
composition changes.

## Why

The current icon reads as a flat pictogram lost in a large dark background. The references the owner
likes (Yams Score, Mölkky Champion on Google Play) are illustrated objects with soft volume that fill
the tile. Instead of drawing a tank, the icon now shows the inside of one.

## Approved direction

[`scene-b2.svg`](scene-b2.svg) is the approved mockup, on the 108×108 adaptive-icon grid:

- emerald water gradient with soft light rays from the surface;
- dark plant silhouettes far back for depth, two pale sage plants on the left;
- one red-orange stem plant (Ludwigia-like) on the right as the single warm accent;
- sand bed with a rock and short grass tufts;
- the ivory fish with sand-coloured fins in the middle, two smaller faded fish further away, bubbles.

Freshwater only: no corals or shells, since the app targets freshwater tanks.

Palette: water `#2b8f80 → #14675e → #0d4741`, sand `#dcbb7c → #b48d4c`, sage `#bad8cb`,
ivory `#f6f4ec`, red `#ef8a5c → #b9483a`, dark `#122622`.

## Constraints for the implementation

- **Visible area.** Launchers only show the central 72 dp of the 108 dp adaptive layers, then mask
  them (circle, squircle…). The mockup uses the full 108 grid, so tighten the composition toward the
  centre: the small fish and the red plant must stay visible in a circular mask. Light polish of the
  drawing (leaf shapes, shading) is fine; the spirit and the palette are fixed.
- **Layers.** Background layer = water and rays. Foreground layer = everything else.
- **Themed icon (Android 13+).** One-colour silhouette: fish, a few bubbles, one plant, the sand line.
  A full scene in one colour is unreadable.
- **Notification icon.** The fish silhouette only; the scenery disappears at 24 dp.
- **Play Store icon.** The full scene at 512 px, saved as `docs/store/aqualog-play-icon-512.png`.
- **Android 6–7.** `minSdk` is 23, but vector gradients need API 24. The legacy launcher icon must be
  PNG mipmaps rendered from the same drawing; adaptive icons (API 26+) stay vector.
- **One source.** Keep generating every asset from one geometry in
  `tools/design/generate_launcher_icon.py`.
- Check the result on an emulator (several launcher masks, themed icons on and off) before calling it
  done.
